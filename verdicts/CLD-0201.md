# CLD-0201: Repeated failed sign-ins: jt-admin@cloudora.io

Engagement: Cloudora · Shift 1 · 2026-08-25
Verdict: False positive · Severity (my rating): Informational (reported Medium)
QA review: Excellent

## Alert
Entra id alert fired for 9 repeated failed authentication attempts on a privileged account, the alert fired at 7:12 UTC Monday morning. the 9 failed authentication attempts belonged to the account "jt-admin" between 06:48 and 7:09 from the IP address 203.0.113.11 which is the London office egress with no success at alert time

## Hypothesis
- if malicious then someone is brute forcing a newly created privileged account before the owner has started logging in and setting up his account. 

- if benign the new starter James Turner is having a hard time with his password on day one


## Evidence checked
- morning handover sheet recorded the new start date for james turner with his account listed as "jt-admin" which was provisioned friday with a temporary password
- help desk _week1.cvs entry HD-5121
- "jt-admin" sign in logs from sigin_week1.csv for source, device and outcome of the authentication attempts

## Action
I closed the ticket since the help desk ticket HD-5121 pointed to the joining process of the new account

## Justification
- every attempt came from the office egress
- every attempt used the corporate windows 11 build
- helpdesk ticket HD-5121 points to Turner calling them regarding his temporary password
- the sign in logs show a successful sign in after the helpdesk call which lead to a verified password reset
- a malicious actor does not call helpdesk to prove themselves and use the corporate IP address and endpoint located in the building to attempt to sign in.

## Tuning note
Tuning Recommendation: Add new-user account setup activity as context in the alert, including recent account creation and related Help Desk activity, so analysts can quickly identify legitimate setup failures.

Risk: Minimal, the alert still fires and requires analyst review; the tuning only provides additional context to speed up the verdict
